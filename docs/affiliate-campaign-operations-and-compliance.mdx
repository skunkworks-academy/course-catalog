# Affiliate Campaign Operations, Measurement and Compliance

**Course ID:** AFF-OPS-301  
**Estimated effort:** 10–12 hours  
**Level:** Intermediate  
**Assessment model:** Operational labs, compliance review and a campaign-control capstone

## Capability outcome

You will be able to run affiliate campaigns through repeatable controls for links, claims, attribution, reporting, records, complaints and corrective action.

## Module 1 — Define campaign controls

Create a campaign register containing merchant, offer, approved audience, channel, link owner, disclosure text, approval date, terms version, measures, review date and stop conditions. Separate drafting, approval and publication for high-impact claims or paid media.

## Module 2 — Use attribution as evidence, not certainty

Affiliate systems can use cookies, referral identifiers, last-click logic and programme-specific validation. Returns, cancellations, fraud screening and attribution windows can change whether a commission is confirmed. Reconcile merchant reports with your campaign record and do not represent projected commissions as earned income.

## Practical Lab 1 — Create an attribution and link register

1. Record a test campaign link, destination, UTM or equivalent parameters, owner and expected attribution window.
2. Document the qualifying event and known limitations.
3. Verify the destination, disclosure and policy links on desktop and mobile.

**Acceptance criteria:** every active link has an owner; parameters do not expose personal data; and the register distinguishes click, pending action and confirmed outcome.

## Module 3 — Govern product and income claims

Use only approved claims with a current source. Testimonials, success stories and case studies need context; they are not proof of typical results. Never alter screenshots or dashboards to imply performance, availability or customer outcomes that are not supported.

## Module 4 — Protect data and customer trust

Restrict access to analytics, email systems and merchant dashboards using named accounts and least privilege. Export only the data needed for a decision, redact learner or customer data in evidence packs, and review vendors that receive tracking or contact information.

## Practical Lab 2 — Perform a claims and privacy review

Review one campaign asset and identify: commercial claims, disclosure location, data collected, third-party tools, privacy link, consent state, evidence source and change owner.

**Acceptance criteria:** unverified claims are removed or escalated; data collection is justified; and a reader can access privacy and support information.

## Module 5 — Build a useful campaign dashboard

Track the full funnel: qualified visits, consented leads, clicks, pending conversions, confirmed conversions, refunds or reversals, complaints, unsubscribe rate and support themes. Label source and reporting period. Report confidence limits and avoid comparing incompatible date ranges.

## Module 6 — Manage incidents and optimisation

Pause and investigate when links break, terms change, a misleading claim is found, complaints rise, consent records are missing or suspicious traffic appears. Use a change record with root cause, correction, approver, notification decision and verification step.

## Practical Lab 3 — Run a campaign-control review

Given a campaign with a changed price and a complaint about a result claim, create a stop-or-continue decision. Record corrective steps, customer-facing changes, evidence needed and a follow-up date.

## Capstone — Affiliate campaign control pack

Submit a campaign register, attribution register, claims-and-privacy review, dashboard definition and incident response record. Include a 30-day review agenda that names decisions rather than merely reporting metrics.

## Assessment rubric

| Criterion | Weight | Competent evidence |
|---|---:|---|
| Campaign control design | 20% | Ownership, approval and stop conditions are explicit |
| Attribution governance | 20% | Outcomes and limitations are recorded accurately |
| Claim and privacy controls | 25% | Sources, disclosures and data boundaries are verified |
| Reporting and response | 20% | Dashboard metrics lead to accountable actions |
| Evidence quality | 15% | Records are redacted, current and auditable |
