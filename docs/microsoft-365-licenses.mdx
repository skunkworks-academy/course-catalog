# Microsoft 365 Licenses

**Course ID:** M365-LIC-101  
**Estimated effort:** 5–8 hours  
**Level:** Intermediate  
**Assessment model:** Licence inventory, allocation labs, group-based automation, billing reconciliation, capstone and evidence of capability record

## Capability outcome

You will be able to interpret Microsoft 365 subscription and licence data, assign services using least-privilege administration, implement group-based licensing, identify assignment failures, reclaim unused capacity, reconcile commercial records and produce an auditable licensing control pack.

## Required environment

Use a Microsoft 365 developer, demonstration or approved training tenant. You require:

- a tenant account with the minimum administrator role required for the task;
- access to the Microsoft 365 admin center;
- optional Microsoft Graph PowerShell access;
- an approved test user and licensing group;
- a secure location for redacted screenshots, CSV exports and scripts.

:::danger Production change control
Licence removal can disable access to services and may affect mailbox, OneDrive, Teams, security or compliance capabilities. Never remove or change production licences without impact analysis, business approval, data-retention checks and a rollback plan.
:::

## Module 1 — Licensing architecture and terminology

### 1.1 Commercial hierarchy

Distinguish these objects:

- **Tenant:** the Microsoft Entra identity and service boundary.
- **Billing account and billing profile:** commercial structures used to manage purchases and invoices, depending on agreement type.
- **Subscription:** the purchased recurring product or service offer.
- **Licence quantity:** the number of assignable seats acquired under a subscription.
- **SKU:** the technical product identifier exposed through Microsoft Graph.
- **Service plan:** an individual service inside a SKU, such as Exchange Online, Teams or SharePoint.
- **Assignment:** the direct or group-based relationship that grants a SKU and selected service plans to a user.

Do not use marketing product names as your only technical identifier. Record the SKU part number and SKU ID when automating.

### 1.2 Assigned, consumed and available capacity

A basic control equation is:

```text
Available capacity = Enabled or purchased units - Consumed units
```

Investigate suspended, warning or locked-out units separately. Do not assume the commercial portal and tenant inventory always use identical labels.

### 1.3 Direct and inherited assignment

A user may receive a licence:

- directly from an administrator;
- through one or more groups;
- through an automated provisioning process;
- through a service-specific or self-service mechanism.

Before removal, identify every assignment source. Removing a direct licence does not remove an equivalent group-based entitlement, and removing a user from one licensing group may still leave another source in place.

## Practical Lab 1 — Build a tenant licence inventory

### Tasks

1. Open **Microsoft 365 admin center → Billing → Your products** and **Billing → Licences**.
2. Record the billing-account context, subscription status, renewal model and seat quantities.
3. Record assigned and available counts for each relevant product.
4. Identify trials, add-ons, duplicate capabilities and subscriptions approaching renewal.
5. Export or document users with direct assignments and users receiving group-based assignments.
6. Record any errors shown under the product's **Errors & issues** view.
7. Create a reconciliation table linking commercial product name, SKU part number, assigned count, available count, owner and review date.

### Inventory template

| Commercial product | SKU part number | Purchased/enabled | Consumed | Available | Assignment method | Owner | Renewal date |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---|---|---|
| Example product | `ENTERPRISEPACK` | 25 | 22 | 3 | Group-based | IT Operations | YYYY-MM-DD |

### PowerShell discovery

```powershell
Connect-MgGraph -Scopes "Organization.Read.All"

Get-MgSubscribedSku -All |
  Select-Object SkuPartNumber, SkuId, ConsumedUnits,
    @{Name='EnabledUnits';Expression={$_.PrepaidUnits.Enabled}},
    @{Name='WarningUnits';Expression={$_.PrepaidUnits.Warning}},
    @{Name='SuspendedUnits';Expression={$_.PrepaidUnits.Suspended}} |
  Sort-Object SkuPartNumber
```

Export only to an approved secure location:

```powershell
Get-MgSubscribedSku -All |
  Select-Object SkuPartNumber, SkuId, ConsumedUnits,
    @{Name='EnabledUnits';Expression={$_.PrepaidUnits.Enabled}} |
  Export-Csv -Path .\m365-licence-inventory.csv -NoTypeInformation
```

## Module 2 — Roles, permissions and administrative separation

Use the lowest-privilege role that can complete the approved task.

| Responsibility | Suitable role pattern | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Assign and remove user licences | License Administrator or permitted User Administrator workflow | Does not require routine Global Administrator use |
| Manage licensing groups | Groups Administrator plus appropriate licensing permissions | Separate from billing purchase approval where possible |
| Purchase or change quantities | Billing role required by the billing agreement | Does not automatically imply user licence assignment |
| Review reports | Reports Reader or approved read-only process | No write access |

Global Administrator is not a default licensing role. Reserve highly privileged roles for exceptional scenarios and emergency access procedures.

### Control requirements

- named administrator accounts;
- multifactor authentication;
- privileged-role activation where available;
- ticket or change reference;
- requester and approver identity;
- before-and-after licence snapshot;
- validation and rollback evidence.

## Module 3 — User-based licence assignment

### 3.1 Prerequisites

Before assigning a licence:

1. confirm the correct user identity;
2. set a valid usage location;
3. confirm available capacity;
4. map job requirements to the product and service plans;
5. check for conflicting or duplicate assignments;
6. obtain approval.

### 3.2 Service-plan decisions

A SKU can contain multiple service plans. Disable a service only when the design is supported and dependencies are understood. Do not disable security, compliance or retention services merely to simplify the user interface.

### 3.3 Graph PowerShell pattern

```powershell
Connect-MgGraph -Scopes "User.Read.All","LicenseAssignment.ReadWrite.All","Organization.Read.All"

$user = Get-MgUser -UserId "learner@example.com" -Property Id,DisplayName,UserPrincipalName,UsageLocation
$sku = Get-MgSubscribedSku -All | Where-Object SkuPartNumber -eq "ENTERPRISEPACK"

if (-not $user.UsageLocation) {
  throw "UsageLocation must be set before licence assignment."
}

if (-not $sku) {
  throw "Requested SKU was not found in this tenant."
}

Set-MgUserLicense -UserId $user.Id `
  -AddLicenses @(@{SkuId = $sku.SkuId}) `
  -RemoveLicenses @()
```

The exact SKU must come from the tenant inventory. Never copy a SKU ID from another tenant or an outdated article without validation.

## Practical Lab 2 — Assign and validate a test licence

### Scenario

A test user requires approved Microsoft 365 productivity services for a two-week training period.

### Tasks

1. Confirm user principal name, department, manager and usage location.
2. Record current direct and inherited licences.
3. Confirm available capacity.
4. Assign the approved SKU through the admin center or Graph PowerShell.
5. Validate that the expected service plans appear.
6. Confirm the user can sign in to the approved service.
7. Record the planned expiry date and removal owner.
8. Remove the test assignment at the end of the lab unless instructed otherwise.
9. Confirm the licence returns to available capacity.

### Acceptance criteria

- the user and SKU were identified unambiguously;
- usage location was validated;
- only approved services were enabled;
- before-and-after snapshots reconcile;
- removal and service-impact checks are documented.

## Module 4 — Group-based licensing

Group-based licensing automates assignment when users join or leave an approved group. It is appropriate for standardised personas such as frontline staff, instructors or developers.

### 4.1 Design the licensing group

Use a naming standard that communicates purpose and scope:

```text
LIC-M365-E3-Employees-ZA
LIC-BUSINESS-PREMIUM-SMB
LIC-POWERBI-PRO-Analysts
```

Record:

- group owner;
- membership source;
- dynamic or assigned membership logic;
- SKU and disabled service plans;
- capacity threshold;
- exception process;
- review frequency.

### 4.2 Important controls

- nested groups do not provide transitive group-based licence assignment;
- insufficient capacity creates assignment failures;
- usage location must be correct;
- conflicting products or disabled-plan dependencies can cause errors;
- group membership changes may not process instantly;
- direct assignments can coexist with group assignments and complicate reclamation.

### 4.3 Graph PowerShell pattern

```powershell
Connect-MgGraph -Scopes "Group.ReadWrite.All","LicenseAssignment.ReadWrite.All","Organization.Read.All"

$group = Get-MgGroup -Filter "displayName eq 'LIC-M365-E3-Employees-ZA'"
$sku = Get-MgSubscribedSku -All | Where-Object SkuPartNumber -eq "ENTERPRISEPACK"

if (-not $group -or -not $sku) {
  throw "Licensing group or SKU could not be resolved."
}

Set-MgGroupLicense -GroupId $group.Id `
  -AddLicenses @(@{SkuId = $sku.SkuId}) `
  -RemoveLicenses @()
```

## Practical Lab 3 — Implement group-based licensing

### Tasks

1. Create or select an approved training security group.
2. Assign an accountable group owner.
3. Confirm test-user usage location.
4. Assign the approved SKU to the group.
5. Add the test user to the group.
6. Monitor the assignment status and errors.
7. Validate the user receives the expected licence and service plans.
8. Remove the user from the group.
9. Confirm the inherited licence is removed unless another assignment source remains.
10. Document processing time and any remediation performed.

### Negative tests

Perform at least two controlled negative tests in a training tenant:

- missing usage location;
- no available licences;
- conflicting service plan;
- user already has an equivalent direct assignment;
- nested-group assumption.

Do not intentionally exhaust or disrupt production capacity.

## Module 5 — Error investigation and remediation

Use a structured root-cause model.

| Error category | Evidence to collect | Typical corrective action |
|---|---|---|
| Insufficient licences | Enabled, consumed and available counts | Purchase capacity, reclaim unused seats or correct scope |
| Invalid usage location | User location and tenant default | Set the approved user usage location |
| Conflicting products | Direct and inherited licence sources | Redesign assignment and remove conflict under change control |
| Disabled-plan dependency | Enabled and disabled service-plan list | Enable prerequisite or select a supported plan combination |
| Group processing error | Group ownership, membership and error detail | Correct membership, capacity or configuration; reprocess |
| Commercial restriction | Billing account, agreement and subscription status | Escalate to billing owner, CSP or Microsoft support |

### Investigation sequence

1. identify the affected user and licence source;
2. capture the exact error and timestamp;
3. validate SKU availability and subscription status;
4. validate usage location;
5. enumerate direct and group-based assignments;
6. inspect disabled service plans and dependencies;
7. apply the least disruptive correction;
8. reprocess or wait for assignment processing;
9. validate service access;
10. record cause, resolution and prevention action.

## Module 6 — Billing, utilisation and reclamation

### 6.1 Billing account awareness

Purchase and quantity-management permissions vary by billing agreement. Record whether the tenant uses a Microsoft Customer Agreement, Microsoft Online Subscription Agreement, Cloud Solution Provider arrangement or another channel. Escalate commercial changes to the appropriate billing owner or partner.

### 6.2 Reconciliation cycle

Run a monthly control:

1. capture subscription quantities and status;
2. capture assigned and available counts;
3. identify disabled, departed, duplicate and inactive users;
4. identify direct assignments outside the standard model;
5. validate add-ons and trials;
6. compare invoice quantities with approved demand;
7. reclaim licences through approved offboarding and data-retention procedures;
8. forecast 30-, 60- and 90-day requirements;
9. document savings, risks and purchase actions.

### 6.3 Safe reclamation

Before removing a licence from a former employee or changed role:

- block or manage sign-in according to the offboarding process;
- preserve mailbox and OneDrive data according to retention policy;
- transfer ownership of Teams, groups, Power BI assets and automation;
- check litigation hold, retention and eDiscovery requirements;
- confirm application dependencies;
- document approval and rollback considerations.

Licence reclamation is not a substitute for a complete identity-offboarding process.

## Practical Lab 4 — Licence optimisation review

### Scenario

The tenant owns 100 productivity licences and 20 analytics add-ons. Finance reports rising monthly cost, while IT reports assignment errors for new starters.

### Tasks

1. Reconcile purchased, consumed and available counts.
2. Identify at least five optimisation candidates using approved test data.
3. Classify each candidate: reclaim, downgrade, retain, investigate or exception.
4. Estimate the capacity impact without inventing prices.
5. Identify users with direct licences that should be group-managed.
6. Identify any upcoming shortage.
7. Produce an action plan with owners and due dates.
8. Prepare an executive summary covering cost, service risk and governance gaps.

## Module 7 — Licence governance operating model

Define these controls:

### Request

- business role and manager;
- required application capability;
- region and usage location;
- duration and cost centre;
- data, security and compliance requirements.

### Approval

- budget owner;
- service owner;
- security or compliance approval for elevated products;
- exception expiry.

### Provision

- standard group where possible;
- direct assignment only with documented reason;
- automated validation and error monitoring;
- user notification and acceptable-use conditions.

### Review

- monthly capacity and billing reconciliation;
- quarterly direct-assignment and exception review;
- joiner, mover and leaver integration;
- renewal and true-up planning;
- evidence retention.

### Metrics

- licence utilisation percentage;
- available-seat threshold;
- direct versus group-based assignment ratio;
- assignment error count and age;
- licences reclaimed;
- exceptions past expiry;
- forecast variance.

## Module 8 — Capstone: Govern a multi-persona tenant

### Scenario

A 120-user organization has four personas: Office Employee, Frontline Employee, Analyst and Administrator. Licences are assigned manually, billing ownership is unclear and former employees remain licensed.

### Deliverables

1. Subscription and SKU inventory.
2. Persona-to-capability and persona-to-SKU matrix.
3. Administrative role and separation-of-duties model.
4. Group-based licensing design for all standard personas.
5. Direct-assignment exception register.
6. Assignment-error investigation report.
7. Offboarding and licence-reclamation procedure.
8. Monthly billing and utilisation dashboard design.
9. 90-day capacity forecast.
10. Executive recommendation with risks, savings opportunities and implementation plan.

### Assessment rubric

| Criterion | Weight | Competent evidence |
|---|---:|---|
| Inventory accuracy | 15% | Commercial and technical records reconcile |
| Persona and SKU design | 20% | Product assignments map to approved capability requirements |
| Automation | 20% | Group-based licensing is controlled, tested and monitored |
| Security and separation | 15% | Least-privilege admin roles and change controls are defined |
| Billing and optimisation | 15% | Capacity, renewal and reclamation decisions are evidence-led |
| Troubleshooting | 10% | Root cause and corrective action are traceable |
| Evidence quality | 5% | Artefacts are complete, redacted and reproducible |
