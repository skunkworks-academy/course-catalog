## Evidence handling rules

1. Use an approved SharePoint, OneDrive, LMS or equivalent evidence repository.
2. Do not place credentials, confidential customer data, personal identifiers or commercially sensitive material in recordings.
3. Obtain consent before recording another person.
4. Redact screenshots and transcripts before submission.
5. Keep the original and improved version of each mandatory performance task.
6. Use filenames containing the course ID, learner identifier, task and version.

Recommended pattern:

```text
art-101-<learner-id>-<task>-v01-<yyyymmdd>.<ext>
```

## Completion package

The final submission should contain:

- the exported Evidence of Capability Record JSON file;
- working links to mandatory artefacts;
- reviewer scorecards;
- the final assessment rubric;
- the learner declaration;
- the assessor decision;
- moderation and resubmission records where applicable.
