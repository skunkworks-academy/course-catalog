- [ ] Evidence and sources are visible.
- [ ] Assumptions and limitations are disclosed.
- [ ] Slides meet contrast and readability requirements.
- [ ] Acronyms are defined on first use.
- [ ] Timing is within the agreed range.
- [ ] Questions and unknowns have a follow-up process.
- [ ] Closing includes a decision and next action.
