# ART-101 Assessor and Moderation Guide

## Purpose

This guide supports consistent assessment of the ART-101 Evidence of Capability Record and capstone presentation.

## Assessment principles

- Evidence must be valid, authentic, current and sufficient.
- The candidate must demonstrate capability, not only describe communication theory.
- Confidential, personal and proprietary information must be removed.
- AI-supported drafting may be used only when disclosed; the learner must still demonstrate personal delivery and judgement.
- A single strong presentation does not replace the required portfolio cycle of planning, practice, feedback and improvement.

## Mandatory evidence

1. Baseline and improved professional introduction.
2. Delivery-control exercise.
3. Technical terminology briefing.
4. Three-level message compression set.
5. Facilitated meeting artefacts.
6. Executive briefing and question log.
7. Business story for two audiences.
8. Professional presentation and slide deck.
9. Difficult-conversation task.
10. Interview response set.
11. Cross-cultural adaptation task.
12. Capstone portfolio and 90-day development plan.

## Assessment workflow

1. Confirm identity and active enrolment.
2. Review the exported Evidence of Capability Record.
3. Test every evidence link and confirm access controls.
4. Sample original and improved recordings.
5. Apply the published weighted rubric.
6. Conduct a verification interview when authorship or judgement requires confirmation.
7. Record the competence decision and reasons.
8. Identify exact resubmission requirements where the result is Not Yet Competent.
9. Send the assessment pack for moderation according to Academy policy.

## Critical failure conditions

The result cannot be Competent when any of the following applies:

- mandatory evidence is absent;
- the candidate cannot verify authorship;
- another person's recording was captured without consent;
- confidential or personal information is materially exposed;
- the final presentation does not include a clear recommendation or next action;
- the candidate invents evidence or materially misrepresents results;
- the overall score is below 80%.

## Verification interview prompts

- Explain how you adapted the same message for two different audiences.
- Identify one delivery weakness visible in the baseline recording and the evidence that it improved.
- Which capstone assumption creates the greatest risk?
- Which question was most difficult and how would you improve the answer?
- How did reviewer feedback change the final deliverable?
- Where did AI or another person assist, and what work remained your own?

## Moderation sample

Recommended moderation sample:

- all first-time assessors;
- all borderline outcomes between 75% and 84%;
- all resubmissions following a critical gap;
- at least 20% of competent portfolios per cohort;
- all appeals and suspected integrity cases.
